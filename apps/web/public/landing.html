08 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Magura a futures or leverage platform?

No. Magura is spot only. You buy and sell real tokens, so there are no perps, no leverage and no liquidations.

Who holds my funds?

You do. Your wallet is created for you when you sign in and trades are signed from it. You can export it or withdraw at any time.

How does copy trading work?

Pick any wallet, from the Smart ranking or by pasting an address. Choose a fixed size or a share of their trade, whether to copy their sells, and filters such as market cap and coin age. Magura mirrors their buys within seconds.

Which chains are supported?

Solana, Base, BNB Chain and Ethereum, all in one terminal with one portfolio.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Magura runs in any browser and installs to your home screen as an app, with push alerts.
